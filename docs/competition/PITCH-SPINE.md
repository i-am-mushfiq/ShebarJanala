# Shebar Janala Pitch Spine

## Positioning

**A safety-constrained civic AI system that turns ordinary Bangla or English life situations into verifiable public-service actions.**

## Ten-minute structure

1. **The human problem:** a citizen should not need to know the programme, ministry, or bureaucratic term before asking for help.
2. **Rokeya's situation:** begin with an ordinary Bangla life story, not a feature list.
3. **The shift:** existing systems start with the service; Shebar Janala starts with the human situation.
4. **The core system:** language/fact-frame inference, retrieval, deterministic rules, grounded response plan.
5. **The safety claim:** AI understands the citizen; rules protect the citizen.
6. **The proof:** live uncertainty, prompt-override resistance, provider-off fallback, and `npm run eval` results.
7. **The action:** Need → Find → Check → Explain → Act → Track.
8. **The close:** sources establish truth and named humans own content accountability.

## Scope discipline

Budget transparency, broad dashboards, beneficiary administration, civic issue reporting, maps, USSD, voice, and the ledger demonstrate that a serious product exists. They are not the pitch narrative. Mention them once as deployment evidence and return to the constrained civic-reasoning architecture.

## Language to avoid

- “AI decides who qualifies.”
- “The AI verifies government information.”
- “Blockchain-style audit proves every record is immutable.”
- “Our synthetic benchmark proves national accuracy.”
- “We built everything citizens need.”

## Closing words

**AI understands language.**
**Rules determine eligibility.**
**Sources establish truth.**
**Humans own accountability.**
